DELETE ACCOUNT.

Account deletion begins with a 7-day waiting period. You can cancel during that period. Once processing completes, you will no longer be able to sign in and your game progress and profile details will be removed.

  • Financial ledger and audit records are retained in pseudonymised form for accounting and fraud prevention. User-entered payout destination labels and user-linked attribution tokens are removed.
  • An unpaid cash balance or open payout request can pause deletion for owner review; this process does not move or forfeit any balance.
  • Deletion is permanent after processing is complete.
