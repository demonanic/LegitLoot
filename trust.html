WHAT IS LIVE RIGHT NOW?

This is a development preview. The public game directory and informational pages are available. Provider integrations, reward issuance, cash withdrawals, daily rewards, quests and leaderboards are not active unless a page explicitly says otherwise.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Can I earn or withdraw money right now?

No. Live earning providers and cash payouts are not enabled in this preview. Do not treat any design mockup or promotional example as a guaranteed earning opportunity.

Are game credits the same as cash?

No. Game credits are non-transferable, have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or converted. Cash rewards use a separate ledger and only become available after validation and settlement.

Are offerwalls connected?

No provider should be considered active until its current terms and approval for the intended reward model have been verified and the integration has passed testing.

Why are some games marked planned?

A title may be featured before its web destination or public release is ready. We will not invent launch links or represent an unreleased title as playable.

Where can I ask for help?

Contact hello@legitloot.site. Do not send passwords, verification codes or sensitive payment details by email.
