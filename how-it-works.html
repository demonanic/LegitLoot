WHAT IS AVAILABLE NOW?

This is a development preview. The public games directory and informational pages are available. Provider rewards, cash withdrawals, daily rewards, quests, and leaderboards are not active.

Clear answers

Can I earn or withdraw money right now?

No. Live earning providers and cash payouts are not enabled in this preview. Do not treat a design or promotional example as a guaranteed opportunity.

Are game credits the same as cash?

No. Game credits are non-transferable, have no cash value, and cannot be withdrawn or converted. Cash rewards use a separate ledger and only become available after validation and settlement.

Are offerwalls connected?

No provider should be considered active until its terms and approval for the intended reward model have been verified and the integration has passed testing.

Why are some games marked planned?

A title may be featured before its web destination or public release is ready. We will not invent launch links or represent an unreleased title as playable.

Where can I ask for help?

Contact hello@legitloot.site. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or sensitive payment details by email.
