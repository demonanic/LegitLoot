TWO WALLETS. TWO DIFFERENT THINGS.

Game credits are non-transferable, have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. Cash rewards are tracked separately and only become available after provider settlement and reconciliation. Never treat a pending reward as guaranteed cash.
