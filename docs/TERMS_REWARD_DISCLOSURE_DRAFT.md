# Draft Terms — Earned Loot, provider verification, and LootCoins

**DRAFT FOR OWNER AND LEGAL REVIEW — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

## Earned Loot and provider rewards

Eligible Hustle activities may generate Earned Loot rewards. The amount allocated to a player is based on the eligible provider-reported award and the reward settings for the offer. LegitLoot's initial Moonetix Split to User setting is 50%; this setting may be changed for future offer credits. The amount shown on the offerwall is the player's reward after the provider's conversion rate, user split, any active bonus, and rounding. LegitLoot credits that player reward as reported by the provider and does not apply a second percentage deduction. Provider terms, eligibility, and the applicable offer conditions may affect whether a completion qualifies. Changes to reward settings apply to future credited steps; previously credited rewards and their matching reversals retain their original amounts.

A provider's completion notice is not final approval. Reported rewards may first appear as pending while LegitLoot verifies the event, checks for duplicate or invalid activity, reconciles provider reporting and settlement, and completes any required review. Verification and settlement times can vary by provider and activity; no fixed processing time is promised unless expressly stated for a particular offer.

Pending Earned Loot is an estimate only. It is not part of the available balance and cannot be redeemed, spent, or converted into LootCoins. A reward becomes available only after the required validation, reconciliation, and approval steps are complete.

A reward may be rejected, held, or reversed where the provider reports a cancellation, chargeback, duplicate, invalid completion, suspected fraud, or other failure to satisfy the applicable offer conditions. LegitLoot will record reward adjustments in its account history. A pending reward that is rejected is not credited as available Earned Loot. If an already-approved reward is later disputed or reversed, LegitLoot will apply its published review and adjustment process; it will not silently alter historical account records. Any recovery from value already redeemed or spent will be handled only under a separately published policy and applicable law.

## Earned Loot value and redemption

The wallet displays available Earned Loot in EL units and an informational cash-equivalent amount. That display does not guarantee immediate payment or mean that a payout method is active. Redemption remains subject to eligibility, the applicable minimum, review and approval, available redemption methods, and any separately announced activation conditions.

## LootCoins

LootCoins (LC) are an in-platform utility currency. They may be awarded for eligible gameplay milestones and challenges or acquired through a purchase using available Earned Loot. LC cannot be purchased with cash or a payment card, cannot be earned from Hustle/provider completions, and cannot be withdrawn, redeemed for cash, or converted back into Earned Loot. LC may be used only for eligible LegitLoot games, game upgrades, merchandise, and seasonal activities.

Limited-time Earned Loot-to-LC packages may offer different LC quantities for a stated Earned Loot price. The price, quantity, eligibility, duration, and any limits will be shown before purchase. A promotion does not create a cash value for LC.

## No promise of provider activity

Provider availability, offer eligibility, award amounts, and verification processes may change. A task shown by a provider is not a guarantee that a reward will be approved. Players must follow both the offer's terms and LegitLoot's rules.
