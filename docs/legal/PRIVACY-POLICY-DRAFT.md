# LegitLoot Privacy Notice — Draft for Legal Review

**Status:** Working draft only. Not approved for publication or reliance.  
**Draft date:** November 24, 2026  
**Operator:** Individual operator (legal name and notice address to be inserted).  
**Proposed jurisdiction:** North Carolina, United States.

> This is a planning draft, not legal advice or a complete representation of every data flow. Before publication, verify actual production telemetry, hosting/CDN logs, email delivery, analytics, game integrations, provider contracts, retention settings, and legal obligations with qualified counsel. Do not collect date of birth or invite outside testers until the final notice accurately describes the implemented data practices.

## 1. Who operates LegitLoot

LegitLoot is currently operated by an individual operator. The operator's full legal name and an appropriate notice address must be added before this notice is published. Privacy questions may be sent to **hello@legitloot.site**.

If a business entity is formed, this notice will be updated to identify the responsible entity and its contact details.

## 2. Information this service may process

Depending on which features you use, LegitLoot may process:

- **Account information:** email address, display name, authentication identifiers, account status, and account recovery/security events.
- **Age eligibility information:** date of birth supplied during registration, the resulting 18-or-older eligibility decision, and the time and version of the age-gate flow. The date of birth is intended to be stored separately from public profile data and restricted to server-side access.
- **Consent records:** account identifier, accepted Terms and Privacy versions, acceptance timestamps, and the registration/consent event needed to demonstrate which notice was presented.
- **Game and platform activity:** game identifiers, progress, milestone/event identifiers, reward requests, and security/anti-abuse events.
- **Reward and provider records:** provider transaction/event identifiers, offer status, reward amounts and currency reported by the provider, validation/reconciliation status, review decisions, and associated audit records. These records may include information a provider sends to LegitLoot.
- **Technical and security data:** information generated by the website, hosting, authentication, and security services, which may include IP address, device/browser information, request metadata, timestamps, and diagnostic logs. Exact collection and retention must be confirmed against provider settings before publication.
- **Communications:** information you include when contacting support.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, government identity documents, or payment credentials to the support email unless a specific, secure process is later announced.

## 3. Why information is used

Information may be used to:
- create and secure accounts, authenticate users, and support account recovery;
- check the 18+ eligibility requirement and prevent underage registration;
- record acceptance of applicable terms and privacy notices;
- operate games and requested platform features;
- validate, reconcile, approve, dispute, or reverse reward events and prevent duplicate or fraudulent activity;
- respond to support requests and communicate service changes;
- protect the Services, investigate abuse, troubleshoot failures, and maintain audit records;
- comply with applicable legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

The final notice must identify the applicable legal bases or other required disclosures for each relevant jurisdiction after counsel reviews the actual data flows.

## 4. How information may be shared

LegitLoot does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be disclosed only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including to:

- **Supabase**, for database, authentication, and related backend services;
- **Moonetix and other offer/reward providers actually enabled**, to receive or reconcile provider activity and administer eligible offers;
- **website hosting, CDN, security, and email service providers** actually used to deliver and protect the Services;
- professional advisers, regulators, law enforcement, or other parties where legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights, users, or the Services;
- a successor in a permitted business transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards and notice where required.

This list is a draft category list, not a verified exhaustive vendor inventory. Before publication, the operator must confirm every active vendor, its role, what data it receives, applicable contract terms, and any international transfers.

Third-party providers may independently collect information under their own privacy notices. Review those notices before participating in an external offer or service.

## 5. Rewards and account status

Reward events may be linked to your account using provider transaction identifiers and account identifiers. A provider callback is not itself approval of a reward. LegitLoot may retain event, reconciliation, approval, dispute, and audit information to maintain accurate records and investigate abuse. Current payouts and withdrawals are disabled.

## 6. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this notice, including account operation, security, reward reconciliation, dispute handling, legal compliance, and recordkeeping. Different categories may have different retention periods. Exact periods and deletion exceptions must be established and disclosed before publication; this draft does not invent fixed retention periods.

## 7. Security

LegitLoot uses access controls and technical safeguards appropriate to the systems it operates, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Access to date of birth and consent evidence is intended to be restricted to server-side roles. The operator should document access, backups, incident response, and vendor safeguards before launch.

## 8. Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about the handling of personal information, and to raise concerns about processing. To make a request, email **hello@legitloot.site** from the address associated with your account and describe the request. LegitLoot may need to verify the request and may retain certain information where permitted or required by law, for security, dispute, accounting, or legal purposes.

Account deletion and the treatment of reward/audit records must be implemented and documented before public launch. Deleting an account may not immediately erase records that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes; the final notice must describe those exceptions accurately.

## 9. Children and age requirement

LegitLoot accounts are intended only for people **18 years of age or older**. The registration process is intended to collect a date of birth and block registration when the person is under 18. This is a self-declared age gate, not independent identity verification. If the operator learns that an account was created by someone under 18, the account should be restricted and the information handled under the documented underage-account deletion and legal review process.

## 10. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as LegitLoot features, vendors, laws, and business structure change. The posted version and effective date should be visible. Material changes should be communicated and, where appropriate or legally required, renewed acknowledgement should be collected.
