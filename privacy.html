1. INFORMATION

Depending on enabled features, we may process email, display name, account/authentication identifiers, profile and status, game activity, wallet/reward records, provider completion information, support communications, security/audit records, and information you submit. Authentication services handle passwords; they are not displayed to staff.

2. PURPOSES

Information may be used for account access, platform operation, game activity, ledger calculations, reward validation, support, security, fraud/error review, and required recordkeeping. Legal bases must be finalized by jurisdiction.

3. VENDORS

Hosting, backend, and authentication providers support the platform. Exact vendors, subprocessors, regions, analytics/cookies and data flows must be verified and listed before approval. Earning providers are inactive; any future sharing requires applicable disclosures and agreements.

4. REWARD DATA

USD and LC are separate ledgers. Cash, if offered, requires verified approved Hustle completion. LC has no cash value, cannot be purchased with cash or converted to USD, and partner offers cannot issue it. Payout and identity-check services are disabled.

5. SECURITY AND RETENTION

Access controls and technical measures are used to protect the service, but no method is perfect. Specific safeguards, retention, deletion, backups, and vendor retention must be verified; no fixed retention period is promised in this draft.

6. RIGHTS

Depending on location, rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection or withdrawal of consent. Procedures, verification, timelines and exceptions require final review. Contact hello@legitloot.site.

7. ELIGIBILITY AND CONTACT

Age/territory safeguards, operator identity, jurisdiction, notice-update process and complaint routes must be completed before launch. Contact hello@legitloot.site.

Approval checklist: verify vendors and data sources; operator and jurisdiction; retention/deletion; analytics/cookies; rights process; counsel review.
