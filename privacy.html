PRIVACY POLICY

Version 1.0-draft · This draft must be checked against the actual production data inventory and vendors before publication.

1. Operator and contact

LegitLoot is operated by Samuel Buzbee, sole proprietor in the United States. Privacy requests: hello@legitloot.site.

2. Information

Depending on features used, we may process account identifiers and email; display name; authentication/session information; consent and policy-version records; game activity; wallet and reward records including EL, LC, status, amount and transaction identifiers; provider offer and attribution data; support correspondence; and device, browser, network, security and fraud signals. We do not intend to collect date of birth through the signup flow; signup asks users to affirm age eligibility.

3. Sources

Information may be provided by you, collected through service operation, or received from integrated reward providers and service vendors when you interact with offers.

4. Purposes

We use data to create and secure accounts; provide games and account features; track, verify and reconcile rewards; administer separate LC and EL records; investigate fraud and abuse; respond to requests and disputes; maintain and improve the service; and comply with law.

5. Sharing

We may share necessary reward identifiers and offer information with reward providers to track, validate, reconcile and prevent abuse. Vendors supporting hosting, authentication, security, analytics or support may process data for us. Information may be disclosed for legal process, safety or rights protection, or a business transfer subject to applicable law. We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. We do not share wallet balances with advertisers except where needed for platform/provider processes described above.

6. Cookies and tracking

Essential session, security and functionality technologies may be used. Providers may use their own tracking technologies when you visit or interact with their offer pages; their policies govern their collection. Browser controls may disable some cookies but can impair service functionality.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to operate accounts, provide features, comply with obligations, address disputes, maintain security and prevent abuse. Retention periods depend on the data and purpose. A complete schedule is being established and should be verified against system deletion behavior before publication. Account deletion does not necessarily erase records we are legally or operationally required to retain.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including access controls and secure transmission where supported. No system is guaranteed secure.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain or restrict processing of personal information, or object to certain processing. Submit requests to hello@legitloot.site. We may need to verify identity. Some rights are subject to exceptions.

10. International processing

Data may be processed in the United States and other locations where vendors operate. Applicable safeguards will be used where required.

11. Children

LegitLoot is intended for people 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from people under 18. If you believe a minor has registered, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

12. Updates

We may update this notice. The date/version will be revised, and material changes will be announced.
