05 / THE LOOT SYSTEM

REWARD
STATUS.

Game credits and cash rewards are different things. LegitLoot keeps them separate so players can understand what they have, what is pending and what can actually be withdrawn.

01 / GAME CREDITSPLAY BALANCENon-transferable game currency

Game credits are for eligible in-platform gameplay only. They have no cash value, cannot be transferred to another player and cannot be withdrawn or converted to cash.

Cash rewards only become available after a provider event is validated, settled and reconciled. Unsettled provider receivables are not withdrawable funds.
