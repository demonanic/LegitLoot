05 / THE LOOT SYSTEM

LOOTCOINS.
NO MYSTERY MATH.

LootCoins (LC) are the standard platform reward currency for approved offerwall activity. They are separate from any future cash-reward feature and are not cash or withdrawable.

01 / LOOTCOINS (LC)PLAYER REWARD BALANCEStandard platform reward currency

Approved Offerwall.GG completions are credited in LootCoins according to the provider's standard virtual-currency model. LootCoins are not cash, cannot be withdrawn and are not automatically convertible to money.

PROVIDER NOT ACTIVE
02 / CASH REWARDSREAL-WORLD REWARDSUSD-denominated rewards, if earned and approved

Cash rewards only become available after a provider event is validated, settled and reconciled. Unsettled provider receivables are not withdrawable funds.

LootCoins issuance remains disabled until Offerwall.GG approves the placement and the signed callback, duplicate-event protection and reversal handling pass tests.
